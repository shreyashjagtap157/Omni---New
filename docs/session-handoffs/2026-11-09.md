## 3. Research and implementation findings

### Managed memory versus affine ownership

The historical managed-memory-default requirement remains OPEN. The Edition 1 reference's affine ownership and borrow direction is preserved and not superseded. No architecture was selected.

Three candidates remain:

A. Affine/ownership-first core with explicit managed domains.  
B. Managed heap by default with opt-in affine/unique control.  
C. Composable storage domains with independent access/alias policy and deterministic resource lifetime.

H1 remains a hypothesis: storage reclamation, access permissions, and deterministic external-resource cleanup may be separate but interacting semantic dimensions. This is not a normative rule.

### Initial memory-domain prototype

research/memory-model/spike.rs models region bulk reclamation, strong reference counting, tracing reachability, shared/exclusive access, and deterministic resource-token destruction independent of storage reclamation.

Exact CI result on b841f4a6: 12 passed, 0 failed.

### Closure/task capture and promotion prototype

research/memory-model/domain_lifetimes.rs models:

- Closure and structured-task captures scoped to a region.
- Rejection of a shorter-lived region captured by an outer closure/task.
- Rejection of region-backed captures by detached/escaping tasks, including move captures.
- Tracing-object roots retained for capture-environment lifetime.
- Logical shared/exclusive access across task IDs.
- Explicit deep-copy promotion of a reachable region graph into tracing storage.
- Preservation of graph cycles and shared subgraphs.
- Rejection of insufficient promotion budget before any partial object publication or ID consumption.
- Reuse of existing tracing targets rather than copying them.
